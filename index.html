About Me

I’m Nildeep Jadav, a Graphic Designer based in Gujarat, India. I am quite more interested in User Experience Design.

Currently (Septmber 2022), I am employed as a Graphic Designer at RightMarc a personalised gift shop for almost two years. My day to day work is to create stunning designes for t-shirts, coffee mugs, mobile covers, social media post for advertisment, brochure and generate imagery for products on website (rightmarc.com).

I have completed my B.Tech in information and communication technology from Ahmedabad university. I have always been interested in computers and stuff, but after admitting to the course, I came to know about the designs and got into it. Then I learn graphic design and other design courses from online platforms such as youtube, Coursera, blogs, Udemy, etc.
