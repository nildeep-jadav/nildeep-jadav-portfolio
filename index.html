About Me

I’m Nildeep Jadav, a Graphic Designer based in Gujarat, India. I am quite more interested in User Centric Design.

I have 3+ years experience in Graphic Design. I worked as a Graphic Designer in Arcedior International Pvt. Ltd. a global sourcing and procurement company. My day to day work is to create stunning designes for our website, products we catered and do shoot and edits for social media posts for advertisment, and handle the website backend and frontend for ecommerce part of our business which is called Arcedior Shop .

Before Arcedior, I worked in a personalized gift shop called RightMarc as a Graphic Designer cum Printing Assistant. My Role here was to create stunning designes for t-shirts, coffee mugs, mobile covers, social media post for advertisment, brochure and generate imagery for products on website (rightmarc.com) as well as to get the prints done on coffee mugs, t-shirts and cushion covers, etc.

I have completed my B.Tech in information and communication technology from Ahmedabad university. I have always been interested in computers and stuff, but after admitting to the course, I came to know about the designs and got into it. Then I learn graphic design and other design courses from online platforms such as youtube, Coursera, blogs, Udemy, etc.
